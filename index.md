Welcome! I’m Junteng Liu, a first-year PhD candidate at the Hong Kong University of Science and Technology (HKUST) in the NLP Group. My research focuses on natural language processing and machine learning, with interests in LLM reasoning, reinforcement learning, truthfulness and interpretability, and hallucination in vision-language models.

This site collects my background, research, publications, and ways to get in touch. If anything looks off, it’s easy to tweak—just tell me what to adjust.
